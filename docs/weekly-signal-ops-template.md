## Why cadence matters

Weekly cadence keeps the radar current without turning it into a constant alert stream.

Repeated observation reveals whether a signal is isolated or part of a pattern. That matters more than the number of signals reviewed in a given week.

Operational consistency is the real goal. A stable review rhythm creates comparable notes, clearer promotions, and better decisions over time.
