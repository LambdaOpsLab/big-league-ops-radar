# First Real Signals

## Why these signals were selected

These were chosen as the first real signals because they cover the core strategic areas Profile A is meant to watch:

- sovereign AI and public-sector partnerships
- enterprise adoption in Europe
- infrastructure and data-center implications
- federal AI policy and procurement
- independent benchmarking and procurement intelligence

They are also all publicly visible, recent enough to be operationally relevant, and specific enough to support human review.

## What patterns they reveal

The first signals show a consistent pattern:

- AI is becoming infrastructure, not just software
- governments are shaping procurement and deployment conditions
- vendors are expanding regionally where adoption is real
- compute, data centers, and sovereignty are tightly linked
- benchmarking is becoming part of model selection and governance

That pattern is useful because it gives Profile A a disciplined way to track change across domains instead of treating each headline as an isolated event.

## Why manual curation matters in v1

Manual curation keeps the first real signals credible.

It prevents the project from drifting into:

- noisy aggregation
- weak source selection
- hype amplification
- premature automation

In v1, the value is not volume. The value is judgment. Each signal is selected, reviewed, and added intentionally so the dashboard reflects a real operating process rather than a scraped feed.
