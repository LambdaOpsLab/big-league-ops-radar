# Strategic Report Template

## Purpose

This report turns reviewed signals and observed patterns into a concise operational summary for Profile A. It is meant to support recurring strategic review, not automated reporting.

## Suggested cadence

- Weekly review
- Optional monthly synthesis for longer-range planning

## How to use it

Use the report after manual signal review and after any pattern updates.

1. Count the signals reviewed in the current period.
2. Note which signals were approved and which became project candidates.
3. Summarize the strategic patterns that appeared across the reviewed set.
4. Record operational takeaways for LambdaOpsLab.
5. Capture risks, uncertainties, and areas that still need evidence.
6. State the next focus area clearly and conservatively.

## Report fields

- Week / review date: when the review happened
- Signals reviewed: total signals reviewed in the period
- Approved signals: signals accepted for operational use
- Project-candidate signals: signals worth possible future projects
- Strategic patterns observed: repeated themes across reviewed signals
- Operational takeaways for LambdaOpsLab: what deserves attention now
- Risks / uncertainties: what remains incomplete, unclear, or unverified
- Recommended next focus: the next operational priority

## How it connects signals, patterns, and decisions

- Signals provide the evidence layer.
- Patterns capture repeated meaning across reviewed signals.
- Decisions track what the team approved, archived, or promoted.
- The report combines those three layers into a short operational narrative.

## Current limits

- No backend or persistence layer
- No automatic report generation
- No PDF export
- No scraping or ingestion pipeline
- No AI-generated summaries
- Manual review and manual editing only

## Definition of done

- A reviewer can copy the report into Markdown or documentation without reformatting
- The report reflects reviewed signals, observed patterns, and decision outcomes
- The report remains static, sober, and portfolio-friendly
- The report does not overstate automation or intelligence quality
