# Decision Trail

## Why decision trails matter

Decision trails show how a signal moved through the radar from capture to review to approval or promotion.

They matter because they make the system explainable. A reviewer can see why a signal exists, why it was retained, and why it became operationally relevant.

## How they support human-in-the-loop review

The radar is designed around human judgment. A decision trail records that judgment in a lightweight way so the review process stays visible.

That visibility helps reviewers check:

- what was captured
- when it was reviewed
- why it was approved or promoted
- whether the signal still deserves attention

## How they prevent hype-driven project creation

Project candidates should not appear without evidence of review.

The decision trail forces each promotion to be explained. That makes it harder for weak signals, repeated hype, or unreviewed assumptions to turn into project ideas without a traceable reason.

## Current limit

The decision trail is static and manual only.

There is no backend, database, workflow engine, or automatic promotion logic. CP12 demonstrates the decision trail concept inside the front end and the curated data file only.
